# Privacy Policy for VaporSlide Polar Rush

**Last updated:** October 2026

**VaporSlide: Polar Rush** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
VaporSlide: Polar Rush is designed as an offline arcade game. We do not require users to create an account, nor do we collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or location data.

## 2. Game Data & Local Storage
All game statistics, high scores, unlocked gliders, and player preferences are stored locally on your device. Clearing your app data or uninstalling the app will reset this saved data.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is safe for players of all ages.

## 4. Third-Party Services
This app does not integrate third-party data tracking or behavioral analytics services.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
